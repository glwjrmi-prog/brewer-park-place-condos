  4. Unless otherwise agreed in writing, the Co-owner is responsible for the cost of the requested alteration and its installation.
  5. To the extent permitted by the Condominium Documents and applicable law, the Co-owner is responsible for damage to the building, Common Elements, Limited Common Elements, or Association property caused by the installation, use, maintenance, removal, or failure of the alteration.
  6. Unless otherwise provided by the Condominium Documents or stated in the Board's written approval, the Co-owner is responsible for ordinary maintenance, repair, and replacement of the owner-installed alteration.
  7. The alteration may not interfere with the Association's ability to inspect, maintain, repair, or replace Common Elements or Limited Common Elements.
  8. Approved exterior alterations may be subject to removal and restoration requirements upon transfer of ownership or when the alteration becomes obsolete, as provided by the Condominium Documents.
  9. Board approval does not constitute a warranty or representation by the Association regarding the product, contractor, installation, structural suitability, code compliance, or performance of the alteration.
